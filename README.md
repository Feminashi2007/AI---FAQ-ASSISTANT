## 📌 Project Overview

AI FAQ Assistant is an intelligent question-answering system designed to provide quick and relevant answers to frequently asked questions.

The system allows users to enter their questions and receive suitable answers through an AI-based assistant. It helps users obtain information easily without manually searching through large amounts of information.

The project demonstrates the application of Artificial Intelligence and conversational technologies in developing a simple and user-friendly FAQ assistant.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- To develop an AI-based FAQ assistant.
- To provide quick and relevant answers to user questions.
- To reduce the time required to search for frequently asked information.
- To create a simple and user-friendly interface.
- To demonstrate the practical application of Artificial Intelligence.
- To improve accessibility to frequently required information.

## ⚙️ Project Workflow

1. User enters a question.
2. The system receives and processes the question.
3. The AI system identifies the relevant information.
4. The system generates or retrieves an appropriate answer.
5. The answer is displayed to the user.
